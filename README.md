# OWASP Sivagangai

Welcome to the GitHub repository for **OWASP Sivagangai**, a local chapter of the Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP). This repository serves as a central hub for our chapter's activities, resources, and contributions.

---

## About Us
OWASP Sivagangai focuses on building a community dedicated to the intersection of **AI** and **application security**. Our goal is to provide a platform for collaboration, innovation, and professional growth to address the challenges of securing AI-driven systems and applications.

---

## What You Will Find Here
- Documentation for chapter activities.
- Resources from meetups, workshops, and events.
- Collaborative projects and tools developed by our chapter.
- Information about upcoming and past events.
